# Audio quirks audit

Every known platform audio quirk, checked against this codebase as it stands on 2026-08-17, with a verdict, the evidence in our own files, and what it would cost to fix. Companion to `docs/qa/AUDIO-TEST-MATRIX.md`, which turns the open items into scenarios a human runs on two phones.

Verdicts:

- **breaks** - the quirk fires and the app has no handling. A user loses audio or loses the session.
- **degrades** - partly handled. The room stays usable but something is silently wrong or unrecoverable without luck.
- **handled** - the code already does the right thing. Listed so nobody "fixes" it back.
- **unknown-needs-device-test** - no evidence either way in the repo or upstream. The first device pass decides.

Severities are about the user, not the code: **session-killing** means the room or the singer is dead until reload, **quality** means it sounds or feels wrong, **annoyance** means a small papercut.

Counts: 54 rows. 8 breaks, 14 degrades, 18 handled, 14 unknown-needs-device-test. Fourteen session-killing, thirty-one quality, nine annoyance.

- [Behavior changes: apps targeting Android 15 or higher](https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-15). Verified 2026-08-17: an app must be the top app or run a foreground service to request audio focus, otherwise the request returns `AUDIOFOCUS_REQUEST_FAILED`. Android 15 shipped 2024-10-15.
- [Manage audio focus](https://developer.android.com/media/optimize/audio-focus). Verified 2026-08-17. The automatic fade-out on focus loss from Android 12, which is the force-fade with no web-visible callback.
- [Freezing on Energy Saver, Chrome for Developers](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/freezing-on-energy-saver), Chrome 133, shipped 2025-02. Verified 2026-08-17. Correction to the original claim worth keeping: freezing needs all pages in the group to be hidden **and silent** for over five minutes with high CPU, and tabs using the microphone, camera, USB or Bluetooth are excluded. A room that is playing music or holding the mic is not eligible, so this only bites a fully silent hidden room.
- [Timer throttling in Chrome 88](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/timer-throttling-in-chrome-88), 2021-01. Carried. Intensive throttling of chained timers, one wake-up per minute after five minutes hidden.
- [Chromium issue 41452188, WebRTC audio stream disconnects after a few minutes or less in background with the screen off](https://issues.chromium.org/issues/41452188). Verified 2026-08-17. The device-dependent screen-off WebRTC death.
- [audio_manager_android.cc, Chromium main](https://chromium.googlesource.com/chromium/src/+/main/media/audio/android/audio_manager_android.cc). Carried. Chromium enters communication mode, and therefore SCO, when an input stream opens with effects enabled, which is why the built-in-mic pin works on Android and not on iOS.
- [Oboe Bluetooth audio TechNote, 2023-02-14](https://github.com/google/oboe/wiki/TechNote_BluetoothAudio). Carried. A2DP and HFP cannot coexist on one link, so opening the mic collapses the profile.
- [caniuse, setSinkId](https://caniuse.com/mdn-api_htmlmediaelement_setsinkid), checked 2026-08 in the earlier pass. Carried. Unsupported on Android entirely.
- Doze and App Standby, [Android power management docs](https://developer.android.com/training/monitoring-device-state/doze-standby). Carried.
